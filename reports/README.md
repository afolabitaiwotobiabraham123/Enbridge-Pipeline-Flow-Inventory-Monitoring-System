# Project Reports

This folder contains the analytical reports produced during the Enbridge Pipeline Flow & Inventory Monitoring project.

The reports will document data quality findings, pipeline flow patterns, inventory movements, and operational insights.

## Reports

### data_quality_report.md
Documents the data quality assessment, including missing values, duplicates, invalid records, inconsistencies, and outliers identified during the assessment.

### pipeline_flow_analysis.md
Presents findings from incoming and outgoing pipeline flow analysis, including trends, variations, and flow patterns.

### inventory_analysis.md
Documents inventory movements, changes over time, and their relationship with pipeline inflow and outflow, where supported by the dataset.

### operational_insights.md
Summarizes important findings, potential operational concerns, and data-driven recommendations.

## Reporting Approach

Each report will follow a structured approach:

1. Objective
2. Analysis performed
3. Findings and observations
4. Business interpretation
5. Conclusion and recommendations

Reports will only contain findings supported by the actual dataset and analysis.
